## The Problem

Most professional trading platforms like TradingView are powerful but expensive or intimidating for students just getting into finance and investing. There's no hands-on tool built for learning — where you can explore real markets, practice trades, and build intuition without risking real money.

## What We're Building

A web-based trading platform that combines live market data with AI-powered insights:

- **Live charts** — real-time and historical pricing charts for US equities, with technical indicators
- **Paper trading** — place simulated buy/sell orders with a virtual account to practice strategies risk-free
- **Price alerts** — set custom alerts when a stock hits a target price or crosses a moving average
- **AI market assistant** — ask questions about a stock, get summaries of recent news, and request plain-English explanations of chart patterns
- **Portfolio tracker** — monitor your paper positions and P&L over time

## Why This Fits OpenOwls

Finance is one of the biggest domains being transformed by AI right now. Owl Street sits at the intersection of real-time data engineering, modern frontend development, and LLM-powered analysis — a rich technical challenge with a product that students and faculty in both CIS and the Fox School of Business can actually use.
