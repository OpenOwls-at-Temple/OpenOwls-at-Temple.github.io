## The Problem

Entry-level CS graduates face a more competitive job market than ever. Most career tools are generic. Temple seniors need something that understands their coursework, their skill gaps, and the current hiring landscape for new CS grads.

## What We're Building

An AI-powered career companion that helps seniors from first application to first offer:

- **Resume analyzer** — scores against real job descriptions, suggests improvements
- **Job match engine** — finds roles that fit the student's actual skills
- **Interview prep** — generates practice questions tailored to the specific role
- **Application tracker** — keeps everything organized in one place
- **Skill gap finder** — shows what to learn to qualify for target roles

## Why This Fits OpenOwls

Our seniors are both the builders and the users. That tight feedback loop makes this one of the most powerful projects in our pipeline — the people shipping it are the people who need it most.
