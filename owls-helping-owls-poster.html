The Pledge

Owls Helping Owls
As a graduate of Temple University's Department of Computer and Information Sciences, I voluntarily make this pledge — not as an obligation, but as a reflection of the values Temple instilled in me and my gratitude for the community that helped shape my career.
  • Mentor Support future Temple CS students through mentorship, guidance, and professional connections — remembering that someone once opened a door for me.
  • Share Share my knowledge and experiences openly with fellow Temple Owls, whether they are just starting out or navigating the next step in their careers.
  • Stay Connected Stay connected with the Temple CIS community — through alumni events, online networks, and informal relationships.
  • Champion Consider Temple graduates for professional opportunities within my network and champion their talents to those around me.
  • Represent Represent Temple's values in my career — integrity, curiosity, and a commitment to building things that matter.
  • Give Back Give back in whatever way I can — a coffee chat, a resume review, a referral, a kind word at the right moment. No gesture is too small.
This pledge reflects my commitment to the Temple community that contributed to my growth — and my belief that Owls are strongest when we lift each other up.
